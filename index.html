How this page is kept up to date

This page collects the next eight days of events from St. Gallen venue websites (Grabenhalle, Palace, Kugl, Theater St.Gallen, Kinok/Lokremise, Kaffeehaus, Theater Storchen, Talhof, Rümpeltum), Eventfrog and, when available, the Saiten culture calendar. Check the venue's own page before you go: times and prices can change. "Time tbc" means the venue didn't publish a start time. A dashed "Unconfirmed" tag means only one source listed the event.

Music links come from the venue and artist sites. MP3 opens an audio file directly, Video opens YouTube or the band's video page, and Listen opens a streaming page such as SoundCloud or Spotify.
